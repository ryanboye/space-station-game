# Science And Exploration Portfolio

Status: new coherent loop; several supporting stubs exist

## Player Promise

Operate a station where location, stable infrastructure and specialist facilities
turn observations and physical samples into valuable knowledge. Science should create
timed opportunities, unusual traffic and infrastructure quality demands rather than a
generic research-point bar.

## Existing Foundations

- Research Terminal and Comms Terminal;
- Observatory room and Telescope;
- Hydroponics and Grow Station;
- Botanist and officer/specialty framework;
- charter system with star, orbit, sunlight, debris and resource properties;
- cargo, cold storage, power, air, maintenance and contract foundations;
- station rating and traffic-family weighting.

These pieces do not yet form a research economy. Do not award research for room
presence or passive terminal time.

## S1. Observation Station

Required build:

- Observatory, 9+ tiles;
- Telescope;
- Research or Comms Terminal in Bridge/office workflow;
- stable power and clear operating status;
- Scientist role, added as a hard physical role;
- later exterior Sensor Array or Antenna.

Operation:

- the charter generates bounded observation opportunities: orbital pass, stellar
  event, traffic survey, debris mapping or communications window;
- opportunity shows time window, required instrument and expected value;
- Scientist reserves Telescope/Terminal and works through acquisition and analysis;
- unstable power, blocked instrument, staffing absence or missed window reduces or
  fails output honestly;
- completion produces named data, contract payment, relationship and/or a selectable
  practical benefit.

The player may ignore opportunities without station failure. This is a portfolio
choice, not a universal maintenance obligation.

## S2. Sample Laboratory

Add room: Research Lab.

Minimum recipe:

- Research Bench or Analyzer;
- Sample Locker for ordinary material;
- Sample Freezer for biological/perishable material;
- Sink/decontamination point;
- Research Terminal;
- Scientist role;
- controlled power, air quality and access.

Sample chain:

```text
survey/exploration delivery
  -> receiving and ownership check
  -> compatible sample storage
  -> lab reservation
  -> analysis session
  -> result/data
  -> archive, return, consume, or dispose sample
```

No sample may teleport from a ship to a global research pool. Sample storage and lab
capacity create visible physical pressure.

## S3. Research Programs

Initial program families:

| Program | Facility emphasis | Input | Useful output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Astronomy | Telescope, Sensor Array, Comms | timed observations | route forecasts, prestige, navigation relationships |
| Materials | Analyzer, Workshop interface | ore, debris, damaged parts | stronger modules, repair efficiency, patents |
| Biology | Sample Freezer, Hydroponics, clean lab | biological samples | crop/medicine improvements, contracts |
| Navigation | Comms, traffic data, simulation terminal | completed traffic records | arrival prediction, automation quality |

Programs should unlock bounded operating advantages or new offers. Avoid percentage
stacks with no visible world consequence. Example: a navigation result may add an
earlier arrival forecast, not merely `+3% traffic`.

## S4. Research Traffic And Economy

Traffic sources:

- survey pods delivering small samples;
- exploration ships requiring cargo/sample transfer;
- visiting researchers using lodging and labs;
- universities/factions commissioning observations;
- distressed science craft needing containment or repair.

Revenue sources:

- funded project milestones;
- data licensing;
- sample-processing fees;
- visiting researcher rent/spending;
- relationship-based grants with explicit obligations.

The player chooses publication for rating/relationships or private licensing for
immediate revenue. Neither result should be random loot disconnected from the project.

## S5. Quality, Hazard And Failure

Lab quality depends on:

- stable power and temperature;
- cleanliness and low contamination;
- correct storage;
- instrument condition;
- staff skill/rest;
- noise/crowding where relevant.

Recoverable failures:

- missed observation window;
- spoiled sample due to storage/power failure;
- contaminated room requiring closure and cleaning;
- overloaded instrument producing poor-quality data;
- dangerous sample requiring quarantine, return or disposal;
- contract delay and reduced payment.

Do not introduce alien outbreaks or catastrophic science events in the first slice.

## Strategic Choices

- observation-heavy low-logistics station versus sample-heavy laboratory;
- publish for trust versus license for cash;
- stable easy projects versus risky premium contracts;
- quiet protected research district versus cheap shared industrial infrastructure;
- station-employed scientists versus a tenant research institute later;
- use research output to improve current portfolio or sell it externally.

## Acceptance Scenario

Complete one observation and one delivered-sample project:

- both require a named opportunity and physical staff session;
- sample is visible from unloading through storage and analysis;
- missed timing or removed power fails the correct stage;
- result produces attributable data/payment and a visible practical effect;
- Observatory visitors and Scientists do not occupy the same Telescope slot at once;
- save/load preserves opportunity, sample ownership and partial work.

## Non-Goals

- No universal technology tree gated by abstract science points.
- No passive research from owning a Research Terminal.
- No mandatory research portfolio for ordinary station progression.
- No procedural technology catalogue before two program families are fun to operate.
